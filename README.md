## About

I am a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of **multimodal perception**, **intelligent civil infrastructure**, and **autonomous systems**. My current focus is temporal understanding in complex civil and construction environments, together with dependable autonomy for mobile robots and autonomous vehicles.

## Research focus

- Multimodal sensing, representation learning, and sensor fusion
- Temporal segmentation and worker activity-state understanding
- Autonomous navigation, motion planning, and decision-making
- Robotics for construction and civil infrastructure

## Selected research

| Project | Focus |
|---|---|
| [**Autonomous Delivery Robot System**](https://github.com/shureduan/Autonomous_Delivery_Robot_System) | A multi-layer delivery-robot system for construction environments, developed in Unity as independent research in CMU Civil and Environmental Engineering. |
| [**TGIF: Multimodal Temporal Segmentation**](https://github.com/shureduan/TGIF-Multimodal-Temporal-Segmentation) | Multimodal fusion and temporal modeling for segmenting worker activity states in dynamic civil and construction environments. |

## Methods and tools

- **Programming:** Python, C++
- **Robotics:** ROS, mobile robotics, navigation, motion planning
- **Learning:** deep learning, reinforcement learning, multimodal representation learning, temporal modeling
- **Simulation and systems:** Unity, Docker, Git, Linux

## Current direction

I am developing research systems that connect multimodal understanding of people and worksites with safe, reliable robotic autonomy. I am preparing for PhD research in robotics, multimodal perception, and intelligent civil infrastructure.
